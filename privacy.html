Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026

Qur'an: Nothing Like It publishes short videos on YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. This policy covers this website and the small analytics tool we use to see how our own videos perform.

The analytics tool

We run a private, read-only tool that signs in to our own YouTube channel, Instagram account and Facebook Page and downloads their statistics. Only the channel's owner uses it. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else can sign in to it.

Through Google's YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API, and Meta's Graph API, it reads:

  • the list of videos and posts on our own accounts, with their titles and captions;
  • totals for each video or post, such as views, watch time, how far viewers watched, likes, shares and saves;
  • totals for our audience by country.

These platforms only provide figures added up across many viewers. The tool never receives the name, account, contact details or any other personal information of anyone who watches our videos. It requests read-only access and cannot post, edit or delete anything.

How the data is kept and used

  • The figures are saved on the channel owner's own computer, and are used only to decide how to make better videos.
  • They are not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The sign-in tokens that give the tool access are kept on the same computer and never published.

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be withdrawn at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for Meta under Settings → Business integrations on Facebook. Once access is withdrawn the tool can read nothing further, and the saved figures can be deleted from the owner's computer. If you believe we hold any information about you and want it removed, message us on one of our channels below and we will delete it.

This website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking. Its fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your browser's standard request information under Google's privacy policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs under GitHub's privacy statement.

Watching our videos

When you watch, like, comment on or share one of our videos, that happens on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook, under their own privacy policies (Google, Instagram, Facebook). We see only the totals described above and the public comments people choose to post.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. For any question about it, message us on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook.
